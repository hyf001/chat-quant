# Charting

Use this skill when the task needs a visual or structured comparison of market data, strategy output, risk metrics, or backtest results.

Prefer clear axes, explicit units, readable labels, and concise summaries. Do not invent chart data; use the available runtime, artifacts, or approved Chat-Quant commands.
